the live experience

One room.
One unfolding present.

Construct of Time Live is built as a continuous audiovisual composition rather than a concert with visuals attached. Music, moving image, performers and light share the same timing and the same architecture. The audience experiences the argument about time before it needs to be explained.

01 · performance

Nine works, one arc.

The score moves through nine relationships to time. Each chapter has its own musical and visual state while remaining part of one continuous journey.

02 · audience

Attention becomes physical.

Scale, darkness, sub-bass and image gather a room into a common perceptual field. The work is designed to be felt as much as understood.

03 · architecture

The stage is the instrument.

Seven screens, performers, light, haze and laser geometry operate as one cinematic structure, adaptable to different venues without losing the work's identity.

stage architecture

A cinematic environment,
seen from the audience.

Three performers inhabit the visual field: bass stage left, electronic drums elevated at centre, keys / synth stage right. The seven-screen architecture carries the moving image while light extends its geometry into the venue.
