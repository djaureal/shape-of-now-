construct of time · live performance

Many people.
One shared present.

nine-part audiovisual work · three performers · seven screens · cinematic image · architectural light
I
together

What happens when a room gives its attention to the same moment?

Music, moving image, performers, darkness and light unfold as one continuous composition. Each element changes the perception of the others, gathering hundreds of individual experiences into a temporary shared present.

For a while, everyone is carried by the same clock.

II
the stage

The film becomes physical architecture.

Seven screens create a fractured cinematic horizon around three performers. Image moves through the structure while light, haze and laser extend its geometry into the room.

III
nine works

Nine chapters approach the same question from different positions.

Measurement becomes rhythm. Memory becomes image. Prediction becomes tension. Repetition changes duration.

Nine works. One movement through time.

IV
moving image

The image does not explain the idea. It allows us to enter it.

We Measure

You're the Wave

Beyond the End

V
why live

Understanding that attention changes time is different from feeling it happen.

Sub-bass reaches the body before analysis. Repetition creates expectation. Darkness removes reference. Silence makes seconds noticeable. Then the performance ends.

Its disappearance is part of the work.
