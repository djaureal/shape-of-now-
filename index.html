before anything else

We spend so much of our lives
somewhere else.

We remember. We imagine. We carry what happened and rehearse what has not. Yet every memory is remembered now. Every expectation is felt now. Wherever the mind travels, experience keeps returning here.

the question

So what, exactly,
is now?

Try to hold it and it has already changed. The present refuses to stand still long enough to be pointed at. Construct of Time lives inside that tension: between the time we measure and the time we actually experience.

one work · two experiences

The same investigation.
Two ways to enter it.

The concept, score, moving image and visual language remain continuous. What changes is the relationship between the work, the architecture and the audience.
