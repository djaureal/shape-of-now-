the work

We live between what happened and what might happen. But where are we now?

The Shape of Now is an audiovisual artwork about the strange thing we call the present. It begins with memory, anticipation and perception, then turns the question into an experience made of image, electronic music, architectural light, spatial sound and attention.

Construct of Time remains the wider conceptual and research framework behind the work. The Shape of Now is the artwork the audience enters.
