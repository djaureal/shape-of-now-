I
together

What happens when hundreds of private timelines are pulled into the same now?

The score, moving image, performers and architectural light operate as one system. Rhythm predicts. Repetition conditions. Darkness removes reference. The audience is not told what time feels like; its own attention becomes the material.

The clock keeps running. The room begins to feel something else.

II
the stage

The film becomes physical architecture.

Seven vertical screens form a physical cinematic architecture around three performers. Image moves across the array as material, not backdrop. Light extends the geometry beyond the screens; sub-bass gives it weight; the performers remain inside the same visual system as the audience.

III

Nine chapters approach the same question from different positions.

Measurement becomes rhythm. Memory becomes image. Prediction becomes tension. Repetition changes duration.

Nine works. One movement through time.

01The premise: what it isopen details ↓

The Shape of Now is a cinematic audiovisual performance built from original music, moving image and live performance. Its proposition is simple: much of life is spent in memory and anticipation, while experience itself can only occur in the present.

The audience does not need a briefing in neuroscience or philosophy. The argument is carried by duration, rhythm, image, silence and scale.

02How it worksopen details ↓

The seven-screen structure turns cinema into architecture. Full-range sound gives the score physical depth; sub-bass is felt before it is analysed. Each chapter changes the conditions of attention: suspension, prediction, repetition, compression, release.

The performers are not placed in front of the visual world. They occupy it. Image, music and light are authored as one score.

03What it sets out to achieveopen details ↓

The work does not try to prove that time is unreal. It makes the audience notice how unstable lived time already is. A minute can expand, vanish, repeat or refuse to end. The show turns that ordinary fact into a shared physical event.

04The audience experienceopen details ↓

The opening removes familiar markers: no immediate beat, no exposition, no spectacle asking to be admired. Expectation is built gradually. At the climax, sound, image and light converge into a single event, then release.

The arc moves from stillness to pressure to release without confusing intensity with meaning.

05Live performance and touringopen details ↓

The production is designed to scale from black-box presentation to festival stage while preserving the seven-screen visual logic, full-range sound, darkness and architectural light that make the perceptual argument work.

Engineering, rigging, screen support, ballast, safety systems and venue compliance remain supplier and venue scope.

06The creative authorshipopen details ↓

Conceived, written and scored by Tony Funiciello. The project joins electronic composition, moving image, performance, spatial design and research into one authored system. Its visual language stays brutalist, cold and architectural: real materials, restrained light, no decorative futurism.
